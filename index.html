Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans at any time? ▼
Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll get immediate access to new features. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What happens after the 14-day free trial? ▼
After your trial ends, you'll be asked to choose a plan. Your data and settings are preserved. If you don't choose a plan, your account goes into read-only mode — nothing is deleted.
Is there a setup fee? ▼
No setup fees for Starter and Professional plans. Enterprise plans may include onboarding and custom AI model training, which are quoted based on your requirements.
Do you offer refunds? ▼
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If TenderIntel doesn't meet your expectations, we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.
Can I use TenderIntel for international tenders? ▼
Yes! TenderIntel monitors portals across 50+ countries and supports 12 languages. Our AI adapts to local tender formats, compliance requirements, and evaluation frameworks.
